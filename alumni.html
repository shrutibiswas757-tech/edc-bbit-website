What EDC does

Activities that help students explore ideas, learn entrepreneurial skills, and connect with the startup ecosystem.

Workshops & seminars

Learning sessions on entrepreneurship, leadership, business fundamentals, and practical skills for turning ideas into plans.

Ideation & pitching

Idea-generation activities and pitch opportunities that help students shape problems into clear, testable concepts.

Mentorship & guidance

Connections with faculty, entrepreneurs, and industry practitioners for feedback and direction as student ideas develop.

Innovation events

Collaborative competitions and events that encourage teamwork, prototyping, creativity, and professional growth.

Startup community

Networking opportunities with founders and business professionals, helping students learn from real entrepreneurial journeys.

From concept to venture

A supportive campus platform for students exploring how a promising idea might become a viable venture.
