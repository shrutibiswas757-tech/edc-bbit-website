About The Cell

What is EDC BBIT?

The beating heart of campus innovation, technology translation, and student entrepreneurship at Budge Budge Institute of Technology.

Nurturing Tomorrow's Visionary Founders

The Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC) at Budge Budge Institute of Technology (BBIT) is an institutional initiative dedicated to cultivating an entrepreneurial mindset among students.

We firmly believe that engineering brilliance shouldn't stop at theoretical exams or campus placements alone. EDC acts as the bridge connecting visionary student ideas with business mentoring, prototype funding, and venture development.

🚀
Our Core Philosophy
"Encouraging engineering students to evolve from employment seekers to bold employment generators."
  • Institutional ecosystem backed by BBIT R&D Cell and the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC).
  • Collaborative synergy with student tech bodies like Coders' Club BBIT and GDGC on Campus.
  • Open to all academic years and engineering branches—from Computer Science & AI to Electrical, Mechanical, and Civil.
🏛️ BBIT Kolkata 🔬 R&D Cell 💡 IIC Ministry of Education 💻 Coders' Club 🌐 GDGC on Campus

🎯 Our Vision

To establish BBIT as a premier regional hub for student-led technological ventures, patents, and sustainable commercial startups that solve real-world problems.

⚡ Our Mission

Provide students with access to industry mentors, pitch competitions, seed grants, legal advisory, and collaborative hackathons that accelerate early-stage prototypes.

🤝 Our Culture

An open, interdisciplinary student community where coders, designers, organizers, and managers join forces to build high-impact products.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Common queries regarding EDC BBIT membership, activities, and opportunities.

What is the primary purpose of the EDC Cell at BBIT?
The Entrepreneurship Development Cell (EDC) is dedicated to transforming ambitious college students into innovative problem solvers and startup founders. Under the leadership of President Ashok Shaw Sir and Convener Yusuf Alam, we bridge engineering and technology with viable business models through workshops, mentoring, hackathons, and incubation support.
Who can join EDC BBIT? Is it limited to CSE students?
Not at all! EDC welcomes students from every department (CSE, CSE-AI, IT, EE, ME, Civil, Management, etc.) and every academic year. Startups thrive on cross-disciplinary teams where designers, engineers, marketers, and visionaries collaborate.
How can core members register or update their designation?
Core members and organizers can register their details using the "+ Register as Core Member" button in the navigation or Core Team section. The profile is instantly added to the live directory and can also be exported as a permanent code update via the "Export JSON" button.
What benefits do I get by joining as a student member?
You get direct priority access to hackathons (like INNOV8), startup ideation bootcamps (Innovation Ignite), exclusive guest lectures from startup founders, official certificates of participation/merit, and potential seed funding mentorship.
Does EDC BBIT help if I already have a startup or prototype idea?
Yes! EDC provides structured mentorship, connects you with faculty advisors in the R&D Cell and IIC, helps you develop a pitch deck, and prepares you for state and national innovation grants.
